Roulette Fortress

Privacy Policy · Effective November 25, 2026

Short version: Roulette Fortress has no accounts and saves your progress only on your device. It shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. It does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service, and we run no servers of our own.

1. Who we are

Roulette Fortress (package com.wigglehunt.game) is a single-player strategy defense game made by Biseo Studio. Contact: efdou100@gmail.com.

2. Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to continue a lost run, double the gold from a run, draw new reward cards, get free gold in the shop or open a free gear box), and full-screen ads at the end of a run (from your third run, at most once every two minutes). The SDK may collect and share with Google: the device advertising ID and app set ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This is used to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and for analytics by Google. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Ad content is limited to a general (PG) rating. If you buy Remove ads, full-screen ads are turned off and rewards are given without watching videos.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can optionally buy a one-time Starter pack, gem packs, and Remove ads (forever). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. To avoid giving the same purchase twice, the app keeps the purchase token on your device only.

4. Your game data

Gold, gems, stars, stages, gear, upgrades, settings and your language choice are stored on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

5. What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, location permission, microphone or camera. We do not use analytics, crash reporting or social features, and we do not sell personal information.

6. Children

Roulette Fortress is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
